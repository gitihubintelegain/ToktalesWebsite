How does a school storytelling programme work?

We discuss pupil age groups, the learning objective and the setting. The programme can include a live story, an interactive communication session, a StoryPlay screening or a Young Storytellers competition.

Can film and live storytelling be combined?

Yes. A story can introduce a destination or idea on screen, then become a live performance, discussion or participatory activity. The combination depends on the brief and venue.
