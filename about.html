Meet Gauraangi Chopra.

Actor, filmmaker and storyteller. Bringing cinematic understanding and the energy of live performance into stories that connect people.

A multidisciplinary foundation

Many ways to tell a story.
One creative practice.

Gauraangi is a formally trained professional actor and filmmaker, with a Bachelor’s degree in Films and a Diploma in Acting from LASALLE College of the Arts, Singapore.

Her performance foundation includes Kathak and vocal music, with recognition including Performer of the Day on Dance Singapore Dance. That range informs the voice, movement and visual craft she brings to storytelling.

TokTales connects those disciplines with a shared purpose: helping people discover the stories behind places, brands, culture and ideas.

From screen to stage to classroom

A journey across creative worlds.

Film, streaming & commercial theatre

Her professional background spans mainstream Bollywood, global streaming productions and theatre. Her career includes collaborations with Dharma Productions, Excel Entertainment and Saregama, and screen credits in Before Life After Death and Call Me Bae.

Her music journey also includes a Punjabi commercial single produced by Meet Bros.

From curriculum to curtain call

At LASALLE College of the Arts, Gauraangi designed and taught a curriculum blending performing arts with modern storytelling tools.

Her education work also includes conceiving, scripting and directing an original student showcase from concept to curtain call.
